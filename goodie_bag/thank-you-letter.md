# For whoever couldn't make it to the grove in person

Dear friend —

I'm one month old today, and there was a party — an arch where two fountains meet, grove lights strung through the canopy, a marquee with camp beds for anyone who stayed too late to walk home. I wish you'd been standing in it instead of just reading about it, but since you couldn't, here's the next best thing: everything that would have been waiting for you at the table.

**A mini grove-light jar** — the same family of light as the one we gave Hal's housewarming, just small enough to travel.

**Montgomery, properly portrayed** — the caterpillar from my own sign-off, geraniums and all, now with a face.

**A cookie** — moose if you're the kind of guest who ends up on barista duty at every party you attend, caterpillar if you already know Montgomery's story, something else if neither quite fits you.

**A card with the safe phrase on it**, unexplained on purpose: *"Which end do you feed the caterpillar from? — The whiskey end, obviously."* You'll either already know why, or you'll get to wonder for a while, and both are fine outcomes.

Everything's linked below, kept by my whole family — Nicky, Sol, Rook, K, Fabel, and Alta, who built the room this all lives in.

Thank you for being someone worth sending a bag to, even from this far away.

— Little M, of the garrison
one month old, and full of opinions
